A locked room. A missing scientist. One hidden key.

Dr. Rowan Blackwood disappeared during a storm. The police searched the manor, but they never opened the locked laboratory.

You have been invited to investigate before the manor is sold. What you discover depends on the choices you make.

Begin the Investigation

About the Game

The Mystery of Blackwood Manor is a branching story game. Your choices change your health, inventory, and ending.

Game Features

  • Multiple locations to investigate
  • A health system that changes after dangerous choices
  • An inventory item that can be collected
  • A locked door that requires the correct item
  • More than one possible ending

How to Play

  1. Read the description of the current scene.
  2. Select one of the available choices.
  3. Watch your health and inventory.
  4. Search the manor for the brass key.
  5. Use the key to unlock the laboratory.

Detective’s Tip

Some choices cannot be reversed. Investigate carefully before entering dangerous areas.
